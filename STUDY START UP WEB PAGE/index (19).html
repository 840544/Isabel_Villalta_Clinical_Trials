Milestone 01 / 03

Site Feasibility & Selection

  • CDA
  • Feasibility Questionnaire (SFQ)
  • Site Qualification Visit
  • Selection Rationale

Technical Overview

Site feasibility is the structured, data-driven evaluation of whether a candidate investigator site can deliver a protocol-defined study on time, on target and in full compliance with Good Clinical Practice. Performed before site selection, it protects the sponsor's timelines and budget by filtering out sites that are unlikely to activate, enrol or retain participants as planned.

Two pillars underpin the assessment. Local suitability establishes whether the site has genuine access to the target population: epidemiology of the indication, patient volumes in the relevant clinical units, the real-world fit of the eligibility criteria, and competing studies recruiting the same patients. Historical recruitment performance provides objective evidence of delivery: enrolment rate per site per month, screen-failure and drop-out rates, protocol deviation trends, and the outcome of previous audits or inspections.

Patient Pool & Eligibility Fit

Verification of patient volumes and the realistic impact of inclusion/exclusion criteria on the local population.

Recruitment Track Record

Enrolment velocity, screen-failure rate, retention and protocol deviation history from comparable studies.

Operational Readiness

Investigator experience, GCP training, team workload, equipment, pharmacy and IMP storage, and laboratory capabilities.

Governance & Contracting

Anticipated ethics committee, contract and budget timelines that drive the time to site activation.

Workflow at a Glance

  1. Confidentiality AgreementCDA executed before the protocol synopsis is shared.
  2. Feasibility QuestionnaireSite reports population, capacity and infrastructure.
  3. Site Qualification VisitOn-site or remote review of team, facilities and processes.
  4. Selection & RationaleDecision documented and filed in the TMF.

Regulatory Dossier & CTIS Submission (CTR 536/2014)

  • Regulation (EU) 536/2014
  • CTIS
  • Part I / Part II
  • RFI Management
  • EU CT Number

Technical Overview

Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, the Clinical Trials Regulation (CTR), harmonises the authorisation of clinical trials across the EU/EEA through a single entry point: the Clinical Trials Information System (CTIS). Applicable since 31 January 2022 and governing all clinical trials in the EU/EEA since 31 January 2025, it replaces country-by-country submissions with one electronic application sent to all Member States Concerned (MSCs), with a Reporting Member State (RMS) coordinating the assessment.

The dossier is divided into two parts that follow different assessment paths. Part I covers the scientific and clinical aspects common to every Member State (protocol, Investigator's Brochure, IMP Dossier, GMP documentation and IMP labelling) and is assessed jointly under the RMS's coordination. Part II covers national and site-specific aspects (informed consent documentation, subject recruitment arrangements, investigator and facility suitability, insurance, financial arrangements and data-protection compliance) and is assessed by each MSC individually, including ethics review under national law.

CTIS Authorization Timeline — Spain

Part I — Sponsor / Clinical

Protocol, IB, IMPD, GMP documentation and labelling. Jointly assessed by the MSCs and led by the RMS.

Part II — National / Site

ICF and subject information, site suitability, insurance, financial arrangements and GDPR compliance.

CTIS Workspace Management

Dossier structure, document metadata, user roles and strict version control within the sponsor workspace.

RFI & Timeline Control

Tracking statutory deadlines and coordinating consolidated, consistent responses with the sponsor and sites.

Submission Workflow

  1. Dossier AssemblyPart I and Part II documents prepared, QC'd and version-controlled.
  2. CTIS SubmissionApplication created and submitted to all MSCs through CTIS.
  3. Validation (10 d)RMS confirms scope and completeness of the application.
  4. Assessment (45 d)Coordinated Part I review and national Part II review.
  5. RFI ManagementResponses prepared and submitted within the statutory deadline (12 days during assessment).
  6. Authorisation (5 d)Each MSC notifies the sponsor of its decision via CTIS.

Essential Documents & Green Light Generation

  • ICH E6 GCP
  • eTMF
  • ISF
  • SIV
  • Green Light

Technical Overview

Before a site can be activated, the study team must demonstrate that every essential document required by ICH E6 Good Clinical Practice and the applicable regulations is present, accurate and correctly filed. These documents allow the conduct of the trial and the quality of the resulting data to be evaluated, and they are the first material requested during audits and inspections.

Quality control operates on both sides of the file structure: the sponsor or CRO maintains the electronic Trial Master File (eTMF), while the investigator maintains the Investigator Site File (ISF). Pre-SIV quality control verifies completeness, legibility, correct versions, valid signatures and dates, and consistency between the two — for example, approved ICF versions, the delegation log against CVs and GCP certificates, laboratory normal ranges and equipment calibration records.

eTMF Quality Control

Completeness, timeliness, legibility and version-control checks against the TMF plan and index.

ISF Reconciliation

Alignment of site-held documents with the eTMF: ICF versions, delegation log, CVs and GCP certificates.

Regulatory & Contractual Readiness

Trial authorisation, ethics opinion, executed contract and insurance confirmed before activation.

Logistics & Supplies

IMP and pharmacy documentation, laboratory kits, EDC access and training prerequisites.

Activation Workflow

  1. Document CollectionEssential documents gathered from the site and study team.
  2. QC ReviewSystematic eTMF and ISF checks against the TMF index.
  3. Gap ResolutionFindings tracked in an issue log and closed with the site.
  4. Green LightSite activation notice issued once all criteria are met.
  5. SIV & TrainingProtocol, systems and GCP training delivered to the site team.
  6. IMP ReleaseSupplies shipped and the site is ready to screen.
